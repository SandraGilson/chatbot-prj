04 / STAY PROTECTED

Everyday safety guide

Simple habits that reduce risk, even when a message looks convincing.

♡
⌁

Pause on urgency

Scammers often pressure you to act immediately or keep a request secret. Stop and verify independently.

▣

Protect your codes

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or CVV—even with someone claiming to be from your bank.

↗

Check the real domain

Look for misspellings and misleading subdomains. HTTPS means encrypted connection, not guaranteed legitimacy.

₹

Refuse advance fees

Be cautious of jobs, prizes, loans, or investments that require upfront payment to receive money.

☎

Verify another way

Use the official app or a phone number you find independently—not contact details in the suspicious message.

⚑

Act quickly if affected

Contact your bank/payment provider immediately, secure affected accounts, and preserve screenshots and transaction IDs.

In India: suspected financial cyber fraud?

Call 1930 promptly and report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
